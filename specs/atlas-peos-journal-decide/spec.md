# Atlas PEOS Journal And Decide

## Intent
Bring the useful PEOS Journal and Decide surfaces into Atlas as first-class pages so Atlas becomes the single daily operating app.

## Behavior
- Journal captures user-authored thoughts, ideas, reflections, questions, and journal notes.
- Journal entries stay private capture until explicitly reviewed and promoted.
- Journal does not store assistant execution logs, operational traces, or automation evidence.
- Decide shows owner decision work from proposals, blocked runs, signals, and reviews.
- Decide supports accept, reject, defer, and close-as-done status transitions for proposal packets.
- Atlas actions remain the execution board. Journal promotion can create an Atlas action after review.

## Non Goals
- Do not reintroduce the full PEOS app shell.
- Do not auto-promote journal entries into memory or tasks.
- Do not redesign Atlas beyond adding the missing pages and matching existing UI density.
