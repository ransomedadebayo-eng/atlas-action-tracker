# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ransomed uses Atlas as the execution board inside AEGIS. The app is used to review active actions, identify focus work, and separate review-gated items from work that can move forward.

## Product Purpose

Atlas exists to make current action state clear and trustworthy. Success means the next action, ownership boundary, review need, and blocker state are visible without turning every signal into urgency.

## Brand Personality

Precise, operational, grounded.

## Anti-references

Do not make Atlas feel like a generic task list, motivational productivity app, kanban toy, or decorative AI dashboard. Avoid gamified urgency, confetti-style completion, ornamental gradients, and vague productivity language.

## Design Principles

- Show execution state before decoration.
- Keep review boundaries visible.
- Separate focus work from backlog browsing.
- Preserve AEGIS as the durable source of truth.
- Favor calm density over urgency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, legible compact text, visible focus states, and reduced-motion-safe state changes. Status should not depend on color alone.
