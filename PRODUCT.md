# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ransomed and trusted collaborators use ATLAS as the active execution queue across personal operations, family logistics, ventures, wealth work, health, property, and music. Agents such as Codex and Claude also use it as a structured work surface, turning transcripts, files, and operational context into tracked actions.

## Product Purpose

ATLAS exists to keep active work real, current, and executable. Success means the tracker shows what matters now, who owns it, what can be done by an agent, what needs review, and what is stale enough to hide or close.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, operational. The interface should feel like a reliable command surface: calm enough for repeated daily use, direct enough to support fast review, and serious about reducing user attention cost.

## Anti-references

Do not make ATLAS feel like a marketing dashboard, a dense spreadsheet clone, or a decorative productivity app. Avoid walls of undifferentiated rows, novelty controls, vague status labels, and surfaces that make stale data look active.

## Design Principles

1. Show execution state before detail.
2. Separate agent work from user decisions.
3. Make stale or blocked work obvious.
4. Keep first-view load light, then reveal detail on demand.
5. Favor familiar controls over invented interaction patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-operable task review flows. Support reduced motion, avoid text overlap on mobile, and make badges readable without relying on color alone.
